# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in this project.

## Our Standards
We expect respectful communication, constructive feedback, empathy, and consideration for others. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and other unacceptable conduct are not tolerated.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or community.

## Enforcement
Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed and addressed appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a participant from project spaces.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant.
